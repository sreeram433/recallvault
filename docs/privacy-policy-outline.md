# Privacy policy outline

_Not legal advice. Product language for the MVP._

1. **Who we are.** ReelVault is an independent personal library. Not affiliated with Meta or Instagram.
2. **Data you give us.** URLs, notes, tags, collections, reminders, pasted captions/transcripts, optional name/email, optional export files, and links you explicitly share through **Save to RecallVault**.
3. **Data we never collect.** Instagram passwords, cookies, DMs, followers, Saved-tab contents unless you upload a file Instagram already gave you. The Android share target never reads anything you did not tap Share on.
4. **How capture works.** User-initiated only.
5. **Original media.** Posts stay on Instagram and may disappear. We do not download or redistribute media by default.
6. **Storage.** Local IndexedDB by default. Optional cloud sync would be RLS-isolated and off until chosen.
7. **AI.** SpaceXAI only after opt-in. Notes omitted unless you check “include my note.”
8. **Analytics.** Off. This starter does not transmit product analytics.
9. **Your controls.** Export, delete item, delete library, visible audit of import/export/delete.
10. **Children.** Not directed at children under 13.
11. **Contact.** Replace with operator contact before production.

Full in-app page: `/privacy`.
